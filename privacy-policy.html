Mindswitch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary

Mindswitch does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. The app works fully offline and has no account, advertising or analytics.

What stays on your device

Your training sessions (exercise, level, whether you trained rested or after exercise, reaction times and results) and your settings (vibration, high contrast) are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app or choose "Delete all data" in Settings, this information is removed.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information or device identifiers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or health data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Medical notice

Mindswitch is a training tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice or diagnoses.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
